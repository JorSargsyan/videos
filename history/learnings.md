# Learnings (rewritten by the weekly review)

Baseline as of 2026-11-21, 30 posts, about 8,500 views total, 0 comments:
- Best YouTube Shorts (700 to 1,050 views) opened with a direct warning about the viewer's next
  trade: "Check your trading setup before you enter", "Your trading strategy isn't always the
  problem", "One bad trade can ruin your funded account".
- Best TikTok (808 views, 47 likes): "Before the chart, check yourself." (emotional state).
- Product-first hooks ("Write your trading rules once") did worst on YouTube (20 to 51 views).
Instructions for now:
1. Open with the trader's own mistake or a warning about their next trade, never the product.
2. Prop-firm / funded-account and emotional-state angles have the best early signal; use them
   in about half the videos.
3. End a few videos with a question the viewer can answer in a comment (comments are at zero).
