# Shugo brief (for script writing)

- **Product:** Shugo (shugo.pro), an AI trading coach / discipline layer. iOS, Android, Chrome.
  Free to start; subscription about $14.99/month. Name means "guardian" (守護); say "shoo-goh".
- **What it does:** you write your own trading rules once; before a trade you give Shugo the
  idea (chart or description) and it gives an honest verdict against YOUR rules and a
  technical-analysis viewpoint (clean setup / reasonable / weak setup / no edge). It tracks
  your emotional state (tilted, heated, calm), flags overtrading and revenge trading, grades
  every journal entry, finds behaviour patterns in your history (for example sizing up after
  a loss), gives a trader personality profile, and has a prop-firm mode that tracks daily
  loss and max drawdown limits.
- **Positioning:** every other tool sells conviction; Shugo is the one that says "no".
  It validates the idea BEFORE it becomes a trade. It is not a signal service and does not
  predict the market. Process over prediction.
- **Audience:** retail day traders, futures (NQ, ES) and prop-firm evaluation traders,
  forex and crypto traders who know discipline is their problem. English speaking; UK, EU,
  US, MENA, South-East Asia. Sceptical of "yet another trading tool".
- **Pains to open with:** revenge trading, sizing up after losses, moving the stop, breaking
  your own rules, overtrading out of boredom, blowing a funded account in one afternoon,
  entering before the candle closes, journaling that nobody reviews, trading while tilted.
- **Tone:** direct, calm, coach-like, second person. Short sentences. No hype, no emojis.
- **Legal:** educational tool, not financial advice. Never promise profit or performance.
